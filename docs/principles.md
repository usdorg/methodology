# Principles

An open rating lets anyone check whether it is honest. Ours will have five parts.

## The five parts

- **Factors and weights.** Every factor and its weight is listed, with the current version.
- **Sources and dates.** Every section of a listing names its source, with the date we checked the state records and captured the website. Facts verified in public records are marked apart from what a company says about itself.
- **How a score adds up.** Every factor, weight and formula is public on the methodology pages.
- **Corrections.** Any business can ask us to fix a factual error, free of charge. We recalculate by the same rules.
- **Change log.** When the method changes, we record what changed and why.

## Rules we keep

- **Payment does not change the result.** Paying does not affect inclusion, order, score or text. Paid placements, when they exist, are labeled Sponsored.
- **Records over claims.** A fact found in a public record counts more than the same fact stated on a company website. A claim we could not check is shown as "claimed", not as verified.
- **Missing is not bad.** When we cannot match a business to a license, it gets no page; other factors without evidence are not scored and their weight moves to the others.
- **The score is our opinion.** It is our assessment based on public records and the company's own website as of the date shown.
- **No technical grading of websites.** We do not score how a website looks or what it is built with. We read it for facts.

## What stays private

We publish factors and weights, but not the exact thresholds we use to spot fake data. Publishing them would make them easy to game.
